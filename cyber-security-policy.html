Cyber Security Policy

The technical and operational practices that protect QaSHE Watchman against cyber threats.

Rev.1 · 23 October 2026Owner: Managing DirectorReviewed at least annually

1.Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the technical and operational practices Group QaSHE CG (Pty) Ltd follows to protect its systems, and QaSHE Watchman itself, against cyber threats — unauthorised access, malware, phishing, and any attempt to compromise the platform or the data it holds. It applies to every system we operate or rely on to deliver the service.

2.Secure by design

QaSHE Watchman's security is built into the platform's architecture, not added on afterward: genuine multi-tenant data isolation enforced at the database level, segregation of duties built into workflow logic rather than left to policy, and secrets — API keys, payment credentials — held server-side, never exposed to a browser.

3.Access control and authentication

Access to QaSHE Watchman requires an individual login; credentials are never shared between people. Access to our own underlying infrastructure — the database, hosting, and payment integration — is limited to those who genuinely need it, and reviewed periodically to confirm that remains true.

4.Password requirements

Passwords for any of our systems must be unique to that system and of reasonable strength. We recommend, and where practical enforce, that anyone with administrative access to sensitive systems uses a password manager and multi-factor authentication where it's available.

5.Malware, phishing, and email security

Everyone with access to our systems is expected to exercise real caution with unsolicited emails, links, and attachments, and to report anything suspicious rather than act on it. We do not send unsolicited requests for login credentials or payment details by email, and we would never ask a client to provide theirs that way either.

6.Network and remote access security

All connections to QaSHE Watchman and to our administrative tools are encrypted in transit. Remote access to any system holding client data is limited to authenticated, authorised individuals.

7.Software and patch management

The platform's underlying infrastructure and dependencies are kept current, with security updates applied promptly once available, rather than left to accumulate.

8.Monitoring

Access to and activity within QaSHE Watchman is logged automatically — every status change and approval carries its own audit trail — which supports identifying unusual or unauthorised activity, not just historical record-keeping.

9.Incident response

A suspected cyber security incident — a breach, an attempted intrusion, or anything unusual — is to be reported immediately to davide@groupqashecg.com. We will investigate promptly, take whatever action is needed to contain and remediate it, and notify affected clients without undue delay where their data is involved, in line with our obligations under POPIA.

10.Employee awareness

Everyone with access to our systems is expected to understand this policy and to apply it in practice — cyber security depends on everyday behaviour at least as much as on the technology itself.

11.Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually, alongside the Information Security Policy, and forms part of the practices being examined through the independent security audit already underway ahead of our ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 certification work with LRQA.

Questions about this policy, or to report a concern: davide@groupqashecg.com
