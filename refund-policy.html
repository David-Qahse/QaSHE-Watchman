Refund Policy

Cancellation and refund rights for subscriptions and store purchases.

Rev.1 · 24 August 2026Owner: Managing DirectorReviewed at least annually

1.QaSHE Watchman subscription

QaSHE Watchman is billed monthly in advance. Because it's a service you access immediately, once you've signed in and started using your subscription for a billing period, that period is not refundable.

You can cancel at any time to stop future billing — email davide@groupqashecg.com with your organisation name and we will confirm the cancellation — and you'll keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

2.Physical products from our store

Your legal right — the 7-day cooling-off period

Under Section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, you may cancel your order for any reason, without penalty, within 7 days of receiving the goods. This is a legal right that cannot be excluded by this policy.

  • Returned unopened, in original packaging: full refund.
  • Returned opened but in original, resaleable condition: we may deduct a reasonable amount reflecting the use or reduced value of the goods.
  • You are responsible for the direct cost of returning the goods to us.
  • Refunds are processed within 30 days of us receiving your cancellation.

To start a return, email davide@groupqashecg.com with your order reference.

3.Digital products from our store

Digital products (templates, documents, downloadable files) are delivered electronically immediately once payment is confirmed. Because the product is delivered — and can be copied — the moment it's downloaded, the cooling-off right does not apply once a digital product has been downloaded, consistent with the exclusions set out in Section 42(2) of ECTA for electronic content delivered with the customer's consent.

If a digital file is faulty, corrupted, or not what was described, contact us at davide@groupqashecg.com and we'll put it right or refund you — this is a quality guarantee, separate from the cooling-off right described above.

4.Faulty or misdescribed goods

None of the above affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act if a product is defective, unsafe, or not as described — those rights apply regardless of how long you've had the item.

5.How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method used at checkout, via PayFast.

6.Contact

Questions about an order or a refund: davide@groupqashecg.com.
