// About

Two entities. One team.

Group QaSHE CG (Pty) Ltd is the strategic and technology arm of a partnership between two entities, built on one simple principle: businesses shouldn't have to choose between hands on technical compliance work and the systems that keep it all governed properly.

We focus on corporate governance, legal compliance, and building the tools, like QaSHE Watchman, that make quality, safety, environmental, and risk management something you can actually see and prove, not just something filed away in a folder.

Our partner entity, QaHSE Services (Pty) Ltd, delivers the hands on technical side: auditing, occupational hygiene surveys, pressure vessel maintenance and inspections, and load testing. Together, we cover the full picture, from the systems that govern your business to the people who inspect it on site.

Watchman is built around exactly that skeleton — which means it isn't limited to the standards listed below. Quoted to fit your organisation, not a flat rate.

Priced individually based on your organisation's size, reach, scope, and complexity — not something that fits a single number for every business. This is the support that strengthens what Watchman gives you, not just the software on its own.

Onboarding Guidance & Mentorship

Hands-on setup and training so your team is genuinely working in Watchman from week one, not still finding their way around it a month in — then ongoing mentorship as your organisation's use of the system actually matures, not a one-off session and goodbye.
