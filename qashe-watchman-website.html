// About

Two entities. One team.

Group QaSHE CG (Pty) Ltd is the strategic and technology arm of a partnership between two entities, built on one simple principle: businesses shouldn't have to choose between hands on technical compliance work and the systems that keep it all governed properly.

We focus on corporate governance, legal compliance, and building the tools, like QaSHE Watchman, that make quality, safety, environmental, and risk management something you can actually see and prove, not just something filed away in a folder.

Our partner entity, QaHSE Services (Pty) Ltd, delivers the hands on technical side: auditing, occupational hygiene surveys, pressure vessel maintenance and inspections, and load testing. Together, we cover the full picture, from the systems that govern your business to the people who inspect it on site.

// Our Platform

QaSHE Watchman: one system, always watching.

The cloud based management system behind "Documented Management Systems" — 17 connected modules covering quality, safety, environment, risk, legal compliance, governance, and the people and processes that actually run your business, in one place, with a complete audit trail.

✓

One place for everything

Nonconformances, audits, risk, legal compliance, personnel, processes, assets. No more juggling a dozen spreadsheets.

✓

Audit ready, always

Every record carries its own evidence and history automatically. Nothing to reconstruct before an auditor arrives.

✓

Segregation of duties, enforced

The person who owns a corrective action can never sign it off as verified, enforced by the database itself.

✓

Real management review, not a scramble

A live, statistical, ISO 9.3.2 aligned management review that pulls its numbers straight from every module — not assembled the night before.

✓

Process performance, measured

Weighted KPIs against real targets for every process that runs the business, reviewed over time, not just the people in it.

✓

Your data, walled off

Every client's records are completely isolated at the database level, true multi tenant security.

Quoted to fit your organisation, not a flat rate.

Priced individually based on your organisation's size, reach, scope, and complexity — not something that fits a single number for every business. This is the support that strengthens what Watchman gives you, not just the software on its own.
