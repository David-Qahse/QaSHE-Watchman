Information Security Policy

Last updated: [DATE — set this when you publish]

1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Group QaSHE CG (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information — our own, and that of every organisation using QaSHE Watchman. It applies to all information we hold or process, in any form: data stored in the platform, documents, communications, and the systems that hold them.

This policy is part of the foundation for our pursuit of ISO/IEC 27001 certification, in partnership with LRQA — our preferred certification body, though we work with any certification body a client already uses. It reflects our current practice honestly; where something is still in progress rather than complete, that's stated plainly rather than implied otherwise.

2. Information classification

We treat information according to its sensitivity:

  • Client data — every record held within QaSHE Watchman on behalf of a client organisation, including nonconformances, personnel information, financial figures, and any evidence or documents uploaded. Treated as confidential, always.
  • Personal information — data relating to an identifiable individual, whether a client's staff member or our own, handled in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
  • Internal business information — our own operational, financial, and strategic information, protected according to its sensitivity.

3. Data isolation and access control

QaSHE Watchman is built as a genuine multi-tenant platform: every client organisation's data is walled off from every other's, enforced at the database level through row-level security — not simply hidden behind a login screen. A client's data is never visible to another client, under any circumstance.

Access within the platform is controlled by role and by module: an organisation's Admin decides who on their team can see which parts of the system, and what they're permitted to do there — view only, contribute, or approve and close. Access to our own underlying infrastructure (the database and hosting platform) is limited to the individuals who genuinely need it to operate and maintain the service.

4. Personal information and POPIA

We process personal information in accordance with POPIA. This policy addresses how we secure that information; it is not, on its own, a complete POPIA notice covering what we collect and why — that belongs in a separate Privacy Policy, [not yet published — add a link here once it exists].

5. Third-party and supplier security

QaSHE Watchman is built on established, reputable infrastructure providers for hosting, database, and payment processing. We assess the security posture of any third party that could access or process client data before relying on them, and we hold our own suppliers to the same standard we're building toward for our own systems.

6. Incident reporting and response

Any suspected security incident — unauthorised access, a data breach, or anything that could compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information we hold — is to be reported immediately to davide@groupqashecg.com. Where an incident affects a client's data specifically, we will notify that client without undue delay, in line with our obligations under POPIA.

7. Business continuity and backup

Client data is held on infrastructure with automated backups. We are committed to verifying that backups can genuinely be restored, not simply assuming they exist and work.

8. Employee and contractor responsibilities

Everyone with access to our systems or to client data — employees, contractors, and consultants — is responsible for protecting it: using strong, unique passwords, never sharing login credentials, reporting anything suspicious promptly, and only accessing the data genuinely needed for their role.

9. Review and governance

This policy is reviewed at least annually, and whenever a significant change to our systems, our infrastructure, or the regulatory environment makes a review necessary. Group QaSHE CG's Managing Director holds overall accountability for information security; this policy, and the practices behind it, are subject to the same independent security audit already underway ahead of our ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 certification work.
