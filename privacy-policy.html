Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect and share personal information, and your rights under POPIA.

1.Who we are

This notice explains how Group QaSHE CG (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), of 61 Goodman Str, Rynfield, Benoni, 1514, South Africa, handles personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). It applies to our website, the QaSHE Watchman platform, our store and our communications with you.

Our Information Officer is the Managing Director. You can reach us at davide@groupqashecg.com.

2.Our two roles

When we are the responsible party

For the information we collect about you directly: your account, your orders, your enquiries and how you use our website. We decide why and how that information is processed.

When we are an operator

For the records a client organisation stores inside QaSHE Watchman, including information about its own staff, suppliers and customers. The client organisation decides what is captured and why, and we process it on its behalf and on its instructions. If you are a client's employee and want to exercise a right over that information, please contact your employer first. We will support them in responding.

3.What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, organisation, role and access settings.
  • Records you or your organisation create: nonconformances, tasks, documents, audit evidence, personnel and training information, and any files uploaded.
  • Responses through an emailed link: the name, email address and answers a client, supplier or third party enters on a response page.
  • Usage and security information: sign-in times, the modules opened, and the audit trail of changes made in the platform.
  • Store orders: your name, delivery address, contact details and order history. Card details are entered on PayFast's secure pages; we do not see or store them.
  • Enquiries: whatever you send us when you email or contact us.

4.Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the platform, the store and support you have asked for (performing our contract with you).
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent misuse and keep an audit trail (our legitimate interest, and the legitimate interest of our clients).
  • To bill, keep financial records and meet tax and other legal obligations (legal obligation).
  • To tell you about services you may find useful, only where you have agreed or are an existing client, and you can opt out at any time (consent, or as POPIA permits for existing clients).

We collect only what is needed for these purposes, and we do not sell personal information.

5.Who we share it with

We use established service providers, acting as operators under written terms, to run the service:

  • Database, authentication and file storage for QaSHE Watchman: Supabase.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages.
  • Payment processing for subscriptions and store orders: PayFast.
  • Email delivery for notifications, reminders and response links.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights. Within QaSHE Watchman, each organisation's records are isolated from every other organisation's, enforced at the database level.

6.Where it is stored

Some of our providers store or process information outside South Africa. Where that happens we rely on providers that are bound to protect personal information to a standard at least equal to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

7.How long we keep it

  • Account and platform records are kept while the subscription is active.
  • After a subscription ends, the client organisation may ask us to return or delete its records. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • Financial and order records are kept for as long as tax and company law require.

8.How we protect it

Our safeguards are set out in our Information Security Policy and Cyber Security Policy. They include per-organisation data isolation, role and module based access, encrypted connections, and an audit trail of activity. If personal information is ever compromised, we will notify the affected organisations and, where required, the Information Regulator, as POPIA requires.

9.Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold personal information about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, where we no longer need it or are not required to keep it;
  • stop using it for a particular purpose, or withdraw consent you gave earlier; and
  • stop direct marketing.

Email davide@groupqashecg.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm who you are first.

10.Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your information properly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, email complaints.IR@justice.gov.za.

11.Cookies and browser storage

QaSHE Watchman uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember simple preferences. We do not use it for advertising or to track you across other websites.

12.Children

Our website and platform are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

13.Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the revision date at the top of the page. For significant changes we will tell our subscribed clients directly.

Questions about this notice or your information: davide@groupqashecg.com
